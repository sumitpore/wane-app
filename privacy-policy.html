The short version: Wane does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

Wane stores a small amount of data locally on your device to provide its features. This data is never sent anywhere — it exists only on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Session History

When you complete a focus session, Wane saves the session duration, start and end times, and whether the session was completed or ended early. This powers the session stats displayed in settings (total sessions and total focus time). This data is stored in a local database (Room) on your device.

Preferences and Settings

Your chosen settings — default session duration, auto-lock configuration (enabled state, duration, grace period, skip window, skip while charging), and onboarding completion — are stored locally using Android's Jetpack DataStore. These never leave your device.

2. Sensitive Permissions and How They Are Used

Wane requests certain Android system permissions to function. Each one is explained here in plain language.

AccessibilityService — App Redirection

What it does: During an active focus session, Wane's AccessibilityService detects which app is currently in the foreground by listening for window-change events (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED). If the foreground app is not the phone dialer, contacts, or messaging app, Wane redirects you back to the water screen.

What it can see: Only the package name of the foreground app (e.g., "com.instagram.android"). Nothing else.

What it cannot do:

  • It cannot read any content on your screen (canRetrieveWindowContent is set to false)
  • It cannot read text you type
  • It cannot perform gestures or taps on your behalf (canPerformGestures is set to false)
  • It cannot take screenshots
  • It does not record or store which apps you use

When it is active: Only during an active focus session. It does nothing when no session is running.

Data sent off device: None. The foreground app name is checked in real time and immediately discarded. It is not logged, stored, or transmitted.

NotificationListenerService — Notification Filtering

What it does: During an active focus session, Wane's NotificationListenerService filters your notifications. Notifications from phone, SMS, and contacts apps are allowed through. Notifications from other apps are temporarily snoozed until the session ends.

What it can see: Notification metadata — the posting app's package name and the notification key — enough to decide whether to allow or snooze the notification.

What it cannot and does not do:

  • It does not read notification text content
  • It does not read message bodies or sender details from notification content
  • It does not store any notification data
  • It does not transmit any notification data off your device

When it is active: The service is registered while Wane has notification access enabled, but filtering logic only activates during focus sessions.

Foreground Service (Special Use)

What it does: During a focus session, Wane runs a foreground service to keep the session timer reliable. Android requires a visible notification for this, which is why you see a persistent notification during sessions.

What it accesses: Nothing beyond keeping the session active. The foreground service type is specialUse (subtype: focus_session_timer) as required by Android 14+ for novel use cases.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

What it does: On Android 13 and later, apps must ask permission before showing notifications. Wane needs this to display the session notification (required for the foreground service) and session-complete alerts.

Motion Sensors (Rotation Vector / Accelerometer)

What it does: Wane reads your device's rotation vector sensor (or accelerometer as a fallback) to make the water animation respond to how you tilt your phone. This sensor data is used only for the animation in real time. It is not stored or transmitted.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

To be explicit, Wane does not:

  • Collect any personal information (name, email, phone number, age, gender)
  • Require any user account or login
  • Use any analytics or crash reporting services
  • Display any advertisements
  • Transmit any data over the internet (the app has no internet permission)
  • Access your contacts list (Wane uses system intents — ACTION_DIAL, ACTION_VIEW, ACTION_SENDTO — to let you make calls and send messages through your phone's built-in apps)
  • Read your call log
  • Read your SMS messages
  • Read the content displayed on your screen
  • Read notification text or message content
  • Track your location
  • Access your camera or microphone
  • Share data with any third party
  • Contain any third-party SDKs that collect data

4. Internet and Network Access

Wane does not include the INTERNET permission in its manifest. The app makes zero network requests. No data of any kind leaves your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Wane does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect user data. There are no analytics providers, ad networks, crash reporters, or cloud services.

6. Data Security

All data stored by Wane resides in your device's private app storage, which is sandboxed by Android and inaccessible to other apps. Wane does not transmit data, so encryption in transit is not applicable. The app follows Android security best practices for local data storage.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All Wane data is stored on your device only. You can delete it at any time by:

  • Clearing app data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Wane → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstalling the app: All locally stored data (session history, preferences) is automatically deleted when Wane is uninstalled

Since no data is ever sent to a server, there is nothing to request deletion of remotely.

8. Children's Privacy

Wane is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since Wane collects no personal information from anyone and requires no account creation, there is no mechanism through which children's data could be collected.

9. Your Rights

Because Wane stores all data locally on your device and transmits nothing:

  • You have full control over your data at all times
  • You can delete all data by clearing app data or uninstalling
  • No data portability request is needed — your data is already entirely on your device
  • No account deletion is needed — there are no accounts
  • You can revoke any permission at any time through Android Settings, and the app will degrade gracefully (explaining which features require which permissions)

For users in the European Union, United Kingdom, California, or other jurisdictions with specific privacy rights: because Wane processes data only locally on your device with no server-side component, your data is under your direct control. If you have questions about your rights, contact us at sumit.pore+uncluttered-apps@gmail.com.
